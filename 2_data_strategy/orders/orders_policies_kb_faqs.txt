ORDER POLICIES — Specialty Bakeries & Pastry Cafés

CANCELLATION POLICY:
You can cancel your order within 5 minutes of placing it through the app or at the counter. If the order has not yet entered preparation, it will be cancelled without charge. Once baking or assembly has begun, cancellation may not be possible. For custom celebration cakes, cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the collection date for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours of collection will forfeit the deposit.

WRONG ITEM RECEIVED:
If you received the wrong item, let us know what you ordered and what you got. We will verify and offer one of the following: a replacement, a refund for the incorrect item, or a credit for your next visit. Please report the issue promptly, ideally on the day of purchase.

MISSING ITEM IN ORDER:
If something is missing from your order, please report it promptly. Once confirmed, we will refund the missing item or provide a store credit. We take fulfilment accuracy seriously and log all reported issues.

STALE OR POOR-QUALITY ITEMS:
If your baked goods arrive stale, over-baked, or not as expected, please contact us. We may offer a refund, store credit, or a voucher for a future order. Photos of the item can help us resolve the issue faster.

CUSTOMISATION NOT HONOURED:
If your customisation — such as a dairy-free milk request, a specific cake decoration, or a dietary substitution — was not followed, please inform us. Once verified, you will receive a refund or credit. If the issue involved an allergen concern, we will escalate it immediately to our food safety team.

ORDER DELAYED:
If your order is delayed beyond the quoted collection or preparation time, we will inform you of any known issues. For delays over 15 minutes, we may offer a voucher or partial refund depending on the situation.

ORDER NOT RECEIVED / NOT READY FOR COLLECTION:
If your order is marked as ready but is not available at the collection point, please speak to a team member or contact support. If confirmed lost or not prepared, we will issue a full refund or priority replacement.

PAYMENT ISSUES:
If you were charged twice or your payment failed, contact us with the transaction reference. Most billing issues are resolved within 3–5 business days. You will receive a reference number to track the resolution.

REORDERING:
You can reorder a previous item through your order history in the app. If a specific item is no longer available or is out of season, we will inform you and suggest available alternatives.

FEEDBACK:
We value your feedback on product quality, service, and your overall experience. Whether you loved a new seasonal pastry or have suggestions for improvement, we listen. Occasionally, we may thank you with a store voucher for sharing your thoughts.

FRAUD PREVENTION:
If we notice unusual activity such as repeated refund requests, we may contact you for verification. Our goal is to protect your account and maintain trust across our bakery community.

ESCALATION:
If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided, you may request to escalate your concern. A senior team member will review the full case and work with you to reach a fair outcome.

ALLERGY CONCERNS:
If you have a food allergy or intolerance, please declare it clearly when ordering. Our bakeries handle gluten, dairy, eggs, nuts, and soy in the same production areas and we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment. If your item was not prepared according to your allergen request, stop consumption immediately and report it to us. We take allergen mishandling extremely seriously and will issue a full refund or replacement and escalate to our food safety team.

STORE EXPERIENCE ISSUES:
If you had an issue at a physical location — such as staff conduct, hygiene concerns, or long wait times — please share the store location and time of your visit. We will route your feedback to the local bakery manager and follow up as needed.

ACCOUNT OR APP PROBLEMS:
If you experience difficulty logging in, updating your profile, or encounter app glitches, please contact us. You may also try reinstalling the app or checking for updates. We will assist with account restoration or report technical bugs to our team.

GIFT CARD ISSUES:
If your gift card did not apply correctly or the balance appears incorrect, provide us with the card number or transaction reference. We will review and adjust the balance if needed.

PROMO CODE NOT WORKING:
If a promotional code fails at checkout, please verify the expiry date, eligible items, and minimum spend requirements. If the code is valid and still not working, contact us and we will apply the discount manually or issue store credit.

ORDER MODIFICATIONS:
If you need to modify an order after placing it — for example, changing a pastry selection, adjusting a cake size, or updating a customisation — please contact us immediately. If preparation has not yet begun, we will do our best to accommodate the request.

RECEIPT OR INVOICE REQUESTS:
You can request a receipt or invoice through the app or by contacting us with your order ID. We will email it to you or make it available in your order history. Invoices for business catering orders are available on request.

UNAVAILABLE OR SOLD-OUT ITEMS:
Fresh-baked items are produced in limited quantities each day. If an item is sold out after you place your order, we will inform you as soon as possible and offer a suitable alternative or a refund for that item.

ORDER HISTORY ACCESS:
You can view your full order history in the app. This allows you to track past purchases, reorder favourites, review collection times, or download receipts.

ADVANCE ORDERS AND CATERING:
Large catering orders and custom celebration cakes require advance notice — typically 3 or more days. Catering orders must be prepaid in full at the time of booking. Please contact us to discuss your requirements and confirm availability.
