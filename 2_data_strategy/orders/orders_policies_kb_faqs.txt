**Orders, Delivery and Returns KB — Cyberport Tech Assistant**

1. **How do I check the status of my order?**
   Give the Tech Assistant your order ID and the customer ID or name used when the order was placed. For your privacy, both are needed before any order details are shared.

2. **What do the order statuses mean?**
   PROCESSING: we have received your order and are preparing it. READY_FOR_PICKUP: your click & collect order is waiting for you in the store. SHIPPED: your order is on its way. DELIVERED: your home delivery has arrived. COLLECTED: you have picked up your click & collect order. CANCELLED: the order was cancelled and any payment refunded.

3. **How does click & collect work?**
   Order online or through the Tech Assistant and choose a store. When your order shows READY_FOR_PICKUP, collect it at the click & collect counter. Bring your order ID and photo ID. Orders are held for 5 days. After that they are cancelled and refunded.

4. **What is a pickup station?**
   Selected stores have a 24/7 pickup station: a locker outside the store. If your store has one, you receive a code by email or SMS when your order is ready and can collect it at any time, including when the store is closed.

5. **How long does home delivery take?**
   Items in stock are usually delivered within 1 to 3 working days in Germany and 2 to 4 working days in Austria. You receive a tracking link as soon as the order is shipped.

6. **Can I cancel my order?**
   Yes. You can cancel free of charge until the order is SHIPPED or READY_FOR_PICKUP. After that, collect or receive the order and use your right of withdrawal or our return policy instead.

7. **What is the 14-day right of withdrawal?**
   For orders placed online, EU law gives you the right to withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason. You may test the product as you would in a store. If the product has been used beyond that, its value may be reduced.

8. **Can I return a product I bought in a store?**
   EU law does not give a withdrawal right for purchases made in a store. As a goodwill policy, we accept returns of unopened products in their original packaging within 14 days of purchase with the receipt. Opened products are exchanged or refunded only if they are faulty.

9. **How do I return an online order?**
   Return it in any of our stores, or request a free return label through your account. Include all accessories and the original packaging if possible. Refunds are made to the original payment method once the return has been checked.

10. **What should I do before returning or repairing a device?**
    Back up your data, sign out of all accounts and remove device locks such as Apple Find My or the Google account lock, then reset the device to factory settings. We cannot process returns or repairs for devices that are still locked to an account.

11. **My delivery arrived damaged or incomplete. What should I do?**
    Report visible transport damage or missing items within 48 hours of delivery, with photos if possible. We will arrange a replacement or a refund.

12. **What warranty do I get?**
    Every new product comes with the 2-year legal guarantee under EU law. Many manufacturers add their own guarantee on top, and the terms vary by brand. Refurbished laptops come with a 12-month warranty.

13. **What is the difference between the legal guarantee and a manufacturer guarantee?**
    The legal guarantee is your right against the seller: if a product is faulty at delivery, we must repair or replace it, or refund it if repair or replacement fails. A manufacturer guarantee is a voluntary extra promise from the brand, often with its own claim process.

14. **How do I make a warranty claim?**
    Bring the product and your receipt or order ID to any store repair desk, or contact customer service. We check the device and arrange a repair, replacement or refund.

15. **Can I buy extra protection for my device?**
    Yes. Device protection plans can be added when you buy a smartphone or laptop. They extend cover beyond the legal guarantee and can include accidental damage such as a broken screen. Terms are explained at checkout.

16. **Can I change the delivery address after ordering?**
    You can change the address until the order is SHIPPED. Contact customer service with your order ID.
