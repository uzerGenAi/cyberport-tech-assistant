Frequently Asked Questions — Specialty Bakeries & Pastry Cafés

1. What is the difference between a croissant and a pain au chocolat?
Both are made from the same laminated butter dough. A plain croissant is shaped into a crescent and has no filling. A pain au chocolat is rectangular and contains one or two dark chocolate batons inside.

2. Are your pastries baked fresh daily?
Yes. All viennoiserie, patisserie, and breads are baked on-site every morning. Availability of specific items may be limited and items sell out during peak hours. We recommend arriving before 10:00 for the widest selection.

3. Do you offer gluten-free options?
Our macarons are naturally gluten-free. Custom cakes and certain tarts can be made gluten-free on advance request. Please inform our team of any dietary requirements when ordering.

4. What allergens are present in your products?
Most of our baked goods contain gluten, dairy, and eggs. Nut allergens are present in almond croissants, macarons, opéra cake, and stollen. We cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment due to shared production areas. A full allergen list is available at the counter.

5. Can I order a custom celebration cake?
Yes. Custom cakes require a minimum of 3 days advance notice. Please speak to our team in-store or contact us via the app to discuss size (6-inch, 8-inch, or 10-inch), flavour, and decoration. Full payment is required at time of order.

6. What sizes do your pastries come in?
Most individual pastries are sold as a single piece. Croissants and pain au chocolat are also available as a box of 4. Tarts are sold by the individual or as a larger 6-inch whole tart. Breads are available as a half loaf or full loaf.

7. Are there vegan options available?
Our sourdough loaf and dark rye bread are vegan. We are developing additional vegan patisserie options. Please ask a team member for the current vegan selection.

8. How should I store my baked goods at home?
Viennoiserie (croissants, pain au chocolat) are best eaten on the day of purchase. Breads stay fresh for 2–3 days wrapped at room temperature, or can be frozen. Tarts and cakes should be refrigerated and consumed within 2 days.

9. Do you serve coffee and hot drinks?
Yes. We offer espresso, cappuccino, flat white, filter coffee, café latte, hot chocolate, chai latte, and matcha latte. All milk-based drinks can be made with oat milk, soy milk, or almond milk on request.

10. What seasonal pastries do you offer?
We feature a rotating seasonal menu: Christmas Stollen Slice (November–December), Galette des Rois (January), Hot Cross Buns (March–April), Strawberry Mille-Feuille (May–July), and Pumpkin Spice Brioche (September–November).
