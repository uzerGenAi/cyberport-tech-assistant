**Product Buying Guide KB and FAQs — Cyberport Tech Assistant**

1. **What products can I ask about?**
   The Tech Assistant covers our smartphone and laptop range: everyday, business, premium and gaming laptops, refurbished laptops, and smartphones from Apple, Samsung, Google, Xiaomi, Motorola, OnePlus, OPPO, Honor, POCO, Realme, Nothing, Nokia, Sony, Vivo and Doro. Prices, specifications and store stock come from the live product catalog.

2. **How much storage do I need on a smartphone?**
   64GB suits light use (messaging, calls, a few apps). 128GB is the right choice for most people. Choose 256GB or more if you record a lot of 4K video, keep large photo libraries offline, or play big games. Most iPhones and many Android phones have no memory-card slot, so storage cannot be added later.

3. **Why do storage sizes of the same phone have different prices?**
   Each storage size is a separate variant with its own price. The catalog lists the price for every available size, and the Tech Assistant always quotes the price of the size you choose.

4. **How much RAM does a laptop need?**
   8GB is enough for web browsing, office work and video calls. 16GB is recommended for gaming, photo editing, programming and heavy multitasking. 32GB is for video editing, 3D work and large virtual machines. RAM in many thin laptops is soldered and cannot be upgraded.

5. **What do Intel Core i3, i5, i7 and i9 mean?**
   They are performance tiers. i3 suits basic tasks, i5 is the best value for most users, i7 suits demanding work and gaming, and i9 is for top-end gaming and content creation. AMD Ryzen 3, 5, 7 and 9 follow the same pattern. The letter at the end of an Intel or AMD model matters too: U is power-efficient for long battery life, P is balanced, and H or HX is high performance for gaming and creative work.

6. **What is the difference between Apple M1, M2 and M2 Pro chips?**
   They are Apple's own processors for MacBooks. They combine high performance with long battery life. M2 is faster than M1, and M2 Pro adds more processor and graphics cores for video editing and other heavy work. MacBooks run macOS, not Windows.

7. **Do I need a dedicated graphics card (GPU)?**
   Integrated graphics are fine for office work, streaming and light photo editing. A dedicated GPU is needed for modern games and 3D or video work. NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 or RTX 4050 is entry level for 1080p gaming, RTX 3060 or RTX 4060 handles most games at high settings, and RTX 4070 to RTX 4090 are for high refresh rates and demanding creative work.

8. **SSD or eMMC storage, what is the difference?**
   An SSD is much faster and is standard on most laptops. eMMC is slower, smaller flash storage used in low-cost laptops and Chromebooks. It is fine for web apps and cloud storage, but not for large programs or games.

9. **What is a Chromebook?**
   A Chromebook runs Google ChromeOS. It is designed for web apps, Google Workspace and Android apps, starts quickly, gets automatic updates and usually costs less. It cannot run most Windows software, so check that the programs you need are available.

10. **Which laptop is best for students?**
    For most students, a laptop with an Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 processor, 8 to 16GB RAM, a 256GB or larger SSD and a 13 to 15.6 inch screen is a good balance of price, weight and battery life. Students can use the Student Laptop Discount (code STUDENT10).

11. **What makes a laptop a business laptop?**
    Business models such as Lenovo ThinkPad and ThinkBook, HP EliteBook and ProBook, Dell Latitude and Vostro, ASUS ExpertBook and Acer TravelMate are built for durability, security features and easy IT management. They often include a fingerprint reader, a privacy camera shutter and longer manufacturer support.

12. **What screen size should I choose?**
    13 to 14 inches is the most portable for travel and university. 15.6 to 16 inches gives more screen space and often a number pad. 17 inches suits a desk replacement. OLED screens show deeper blacks and stronger colours, which is good for films and photo editing.

13. **What is a 2-in-1 or convertible laptop?**
    A 2-in-1 has a touchscreen and either a hinge that folds back 360 degrees or a detachable keyboard, so it can be used as a tablet. Examples include the HP ENVY x360, HP Spectre x360 and Microsoft Surface Pro. Look for "touchscreen: true" in the product details.

14. **What does refurbished mean?**
    Refurbished laptops are pre-owned or returned devices that have been tested, cleaned, repaired where needed and reset to factory settings. They are sold at a lower price, show "condition: Refurbished" in the catalog, and come with a 12-month warranty.

15. **Do new phones come with a charger?**
    Many manufacturers no longer include a charger in the box, only a USB cable. Since 28 December 2024, new phones sold in the EU must have a USB-C charging port, and the same rule applies to new laptops from 28 April 2026. iPhone models up to the iPhone 14 use Apple's Lightning port.

16. **What is the EU energy label on smartphones?**
    Since 20 June 2025, smartphones and tablets sold in the EU carry an energy label. It shows an energy efficiency class from A to G, battery life per charge, the number of charge cycles the battery is rated for, a repairability class from A to E, and drop and dust and water resistance.

17. **What is a foldable phone?**
    A foldable phone, such as the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip4, has a flexible screen that folds in half. Flip-style phones fold into a small square. Foldables cost more than standard phones of similar performance and need a little more care with the screen.

18. **Can I connect a laptop to an external monitor?**
    Most laptops have an HDMI port for a monitor or TV. Many also support monitors over USB-C with DisplayPort or Thunderbolt, which can carry video, data and charging over one cable. Check the laptop's ports before buying an adapter or docking station.

19. **How do I check if a product is in stock at my store?**
    Ask the Tech Assistant for the product and the store. Stock is held per store in the product catalog, so the assistant can tell you whether a product is available in a specific store and how many units are left.

20. **Are prices the same online and in store?**
    Prices in the catalog apply both online and in store. Promotions can change the final price at checkout. Ask the Tech Assistant about current promotions.
