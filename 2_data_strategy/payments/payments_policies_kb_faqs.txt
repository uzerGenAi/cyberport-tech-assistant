**Payments Support KB and FAQs — Specialty Bakeries & Pastry Cafés**

1. **Can I pay with cash at the bakery?**
   Yes, we accept cash at all store locations. Please have the correct amount ready where possible as change availability may be limited during busy periods.

2. **What digital payments do you support?**
   We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), as well as Apple Pay, Google Pay, and other NFC contactless payment methods.

3. **Can I pay online and collect in store?**
   Yes. You can place and pay for your order online or via the app and select click-and-collect at your preferred bakery location. Your order will be prepared and ready at the agreed collection time.

4. **Do you offer refunds for incorrect orders?**
   Yes. If there is an issue with your order, please contact us within 24 hours of collection or delivery. Verified issues will be refunded to your original payment method within 3–5 business days.

5. **Can I split a payment between two methods?**
   Yes. In-store you can split payments between two cards or a combination of cash and card. Please inform the team member at the till before payment is processed.

6. **Is tipping supported on card payments?**
   Yes. You will be prompted to add a gratuity when completing a card payment in-store. Tipping is entirely optional but very much appreciated by our team.

7. **Can I use gift cards to pay?**
   Yes. We accept both physical and digital gift cards issued by our bakeries. Enter the code at checkout in the app or present the physical card in-store.

8. **Why was my card declined?**
   Your card may be declined due to insufficient funds, incorrect billing details, or bank-side fraud prevention. Please try a different payment method or contact your bank directly.

9. **Do you store my payment information?**
   No. We never store your card details. All transactions are processed securely by our certified payment provider. We are PCI-DSS compliant.

10. **Can I receive a receipt by email or SMS?**
    Yes. Digital receipts are available after every purchase. You can opt to receive one via email or SMS at the point of sale or through the app settings.

11. **Can I use loyalty points to pay for my order?**
    Yes. If you are enrolled in our Pastry Rewards programme, you can redeem accumulated points for discounts at checkout, either in-store or through the app.

12. **What happens if I am charged twice?**
    If you notice a duplicate charge, please contact us immediately with your transaction reference. Once verified, we will issue a full refund for the extra charge within 3–5 business days.

13. **Is there a minimum purchase amount for card payments?**
    No. There is no minimum spend required when paying by card or digital wallet.

14. **Do you charge any transaction or service fees?**
    No. The price you see is the price you pay. We do not add service charges, surcharges, or booking fees to orders placed via the app.

15. **How do I update my saved payment method in the app?**
    Go to Account → Payment Methods in the app. You can add, update, or securely delete saved payment methods from there.

16. **Can I cancel an order and get my money back?**
    Yes. For standard orders, cancellations are allowed within 5 minutes of placing the order. For custom celebration cakes, cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the collection date. Refunds are processed to your original payment method.

17. **Are prepaid cards accepted?**
    Yes. Most prepaid Visa, Mastercard, or Amex cards are accepted just like regular credit or debit cards.

18. **Do you accept mobile wallets like Samsung Pay?**
    Yes. We accept Samsung Pay and any other mobile wallet that supports NFC contactless transactions.

19. **What if my gift card does not work?**
    Please check that the balance is valid and the code has been entered correctly. If the problem persists, contact our support team with the card number and we will investigate and adjust the balance if needed.

20. **Is full prepayment required for catering and custom cake orders?**
    Yes. All catering orders and custom celebration cakes must be paid in full at the time of booking to confirm your order. This allows us to source ingredients and allocate production time. The deposit is non-refundable for cancellations within 48 hours of the collection date.

21. **Do you offer business invoices?**
    Yes. For corporate catering orders or B2B accounts, we can issue a full VAT invoice. Please contact us in advance to set up a business account.

22. **What currency do you accept for in-store payments?**
    We accept payment in Euros (€) at all of our European locations. We do not accept foreign currency cash in-store.
