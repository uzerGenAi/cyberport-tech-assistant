**Payments Support KB and FAQs — Cyberport Tech Assistant**

1. **Which payment methods do you accept?**
   Online: Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit and debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank transfer (prepayment). In store: cash, debit cards (girocard in Germany), credit cards and contactless payment with Apple Pay and Google Pay. All prices are in euros (€) and include VAT.

2. **Can I pay when I collect a click & collect order?**
   Yes. You can pay online when you order or pay at the click & collect counter when you pick it up.

3. **Do you offer financing or instalment payments?**
   Yes. Purchases of €299 or more can be spread over 6, 12 or 24 months at 0% interest through our financing partner. You apply online at checkout or in store, and the partner bank runs a credit check. The Tech Assistant can explain how financing works but cannot tell you whether you will be approved or advise you on whether to take credit.

4. **What do I need to apply for financing?**
   You must be 18 or over and live in Germany or Austria. You need a valid ID, a bank account in your name (IBAN) and a contact address. Never share your IBAN or card details in the chat. Enter them only in the secure application form.

5. **How long do refunds take?**
   Once a return has been checked, the refund is sent to your original payment method. Card and PayPal refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days. Bank transfer refunds take up to 5 business days.

6. **Can I get an invoice with VAT for my company?**
   Yes. Business customers can enter the company name and VAT ID at checkout and will receive an invoice showing VAT. For in-store purchases, ask at the till for a company invoice.

7. **Can I pay with a gift card?**
   Yes. Gift cards can be used online and in store, together with another payment method if the gift card does not cover the full amount. Gift cards are valid for 3 years from the end of the year they were bought.

8. **Can I split a payment between two methods?**
   In store you can combine cash, a card and a gift card. Online you can combine a gift card with one other payment method.

9. **Why was my card declined?**
   A card can be declined because of insufficient funds, an expired card, wrong card details, a spending limit, or a failed 3-D Secure check with your bank. Try another payment method or contact your bank.

10. **Do you store my card details?**
    No. Card payments are processed by a PCI DSS certified payment provider, and we never store full card numbers. Online card payments use 3-D Secure authentication from your bank.

11. **I was charged twice. What should I do?**
    Contact customer service with your order ID. Often the second amount is only a temporary authorisation that your bank releases within a few days. If you were actually charged twice, we refund the duplicate payment.

12. **Can I use a voucher or promo code?**
    Yes. Enter one promo code per order at checkout, online or in store. Discounts without a code, such as the Accessory Bundle Deal, are applied automatically. Ask the Tech Assistant about current promotions.

13. **Is there a minimum order value for card payments?**
    No. There is no minimum amount for card or digital wallet payments.

14. **Can I pay with cryptocurrency or buy now and pay later in store?**
    No. Cryptocurrency is not accepted. In store, the 0% financing offer is the only way to pay in instalments.
