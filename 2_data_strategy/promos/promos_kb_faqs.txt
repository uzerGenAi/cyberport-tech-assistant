PROMOTIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE — Specialty Bakeries & Pastry Cafés

FRESH PASTRY TUESDAYS:
Every Tuesday, customers receive a free plain croissant with any hot drink purchase. The offer applies in-store and via the app (use code TUESDAYCROISSANT at checkout). One croissant per transaction. Cannot be combined with other discounts.

PASTRY REWARDS — DOUBLE POINTS WEEKEND:
Pastry Rewards members earn double (2×) loyalty points on all purchases over €15 every Saturday and Sunday. Points are automatically credited to your account within 24 hours of purchase. No promo code required. Membership is free and available through the app.

HOW PASTRY REWARDS WORKS:
Every €1 spent earns 1 Pastry Reward point. Points can be redeemed at checkout at a rate of 100 points = €1 discount. Points do not expire as long as you make at least one purchase per 12 months. Bonus points events are announced in-app and by email newsletter.

NEW CUSTOMER WELCOME OFFER:
First-time customers receive €5 off their first order of €15 or more when they register in the app and use code WELCOME5. Valid once per customer. Cannot be combined with other codes.

STUDENT DISCOUNT:
Students receive 10% off their entire order. Present a valid student ID in-store or use code STUDENT10 in the app. Valid during the academic year (September–July). Applicable on all products including seasonal items.

BIRTHDAY CAKE OFFER:
Pastry Rewards members receive a complimentary 6-inch celebration cake slice during their birthday month. The offer is automatically applied to the member's account. No code required. Members must be logged into the app at checkout to redeem.

OFFICE CATERING BUNDLE:
Any catering order for 10 or more people includes a complimentary box of 12 assorted macarons. Use code CATERINGBONUS when placing your catering order via the app or by contacting your nearest bakery directly. Valid for advance catering orders with a minimum 3-day lead time.

SEASONAL PASTRY LAUNCH — 20% OFF:
During the first week of each new seasonal pastry launch, all new seasonal items are available at 20% off. Use code SEASONLAUNCH at checkout. Applicable once per customer per seasonal launch. Seasonal launches include: January (Galette des Rois), March–April (Hot Cross Buns), May (Strawberry Mille-Feuille), September (Pumpkin Spice Brioche), November (Christmas Stollen).

TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
- Promotions cannot be combined unless explicitly stated.
- Loyalty point redemption is not available alongside other discount codes in the same transaction.
- All promotions are subject to product availability.
- Promotional prices apply to the listed items only. Customisations and upgrades may incur additional charges.
- Specialty Bakeries & Pastry Cafés reserves the right to modify or withdraw promotions at any time.
- Gift cards and store credits are not eligible for additional promotional discounts.
- Franchise-operated locations may have limited participation in certain promotions. The app will notify you if a selected store cannot honour a specific offer.
