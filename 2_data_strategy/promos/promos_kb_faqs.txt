PROMOTIONS KNOWLEDGE BASE — Cyberport Tech Assistant

TRADE-IN BONUS:
Trade in your old smartphone or laptop and get an extra €50 on top of its trade-in value when you buy a new smartphone or laptop over €400. Use code TRADEIN50. The trade-in value depends on the model, its age and its condition, and is confirmed by the store team after a short check. The device must switch on, must not be locked to an account (Apple Find My or the Google account lock must be off), and must be reset to factory settings. The trade-in value is paid as a discount on the new purchase. One trade-in bonus per new device.

HOW DEVICE CONDITION IS GRADED FOR TRADE-IN:
Like new: no visible marks, everything works. Good: light signs of use, everything works. Fair: visible scratches or dents, everything works. Defective: cracked screen, faulty battery or other faults. Defective devices can still be traded in at a lower value or recycled free of charge.

CYBERPORT CUSTOMER ACCOUNT:
A free Cyberport customer account gives you order tracking, digital invoices, your warranty documents in one place and faster checkout. You can create one online or ask the store team. Your customer number is shown in the account and on every invoice. The account can be closed at any time.

ACCESSORY BUNDLE DEAL:
Get 10% off cases, chargers and screen protectors bought together with a new smartphone when you order with your Cyberport customer account. The discount is applied automatically at checkout. No promo code needed.

NEWSLETTER WELCOME OFFER:
New newsletter subscribers get €10 off their first online order of €100 or more with code WELCOME10. Valid once per customer.

BUSINESS VOLUME DISCOUNT:
Business customers get 5% off orders of five or more laptops with code BUSINESS5. The order must include a company name and VAT ID. Business customers receive an invoice showing VAT and can ask the business team for bulk quotes, device setup and leasing options.

STUDENT LAPTOP DISCOUNT:
Students get 10% off any new laptop, up to a maximum of €150, with code STUDENT10. A valid student ID or a university email address is required. Valid from 1 September 2026 to 31 July 2027. Refurbished laptops are excluded.

GAMING LAPTOP BUNDLE:
Get a free gaming headset with any gaming laptop over €1,000 using code GAMERBUNDLE. Valid from 1 October to 31 December 2026, while stocks last.

REFURBISHED WEEK:
An extra 5% off all refurbished laptops with code REFURB5, from 1 to 15 November 2026.

0% FINANCING:
Purchases of €299 or more can be spread over 6, 12 or 24 months at 0% interest, subject to a credit check by our financing partner. Customers must be 18 or over and live in Germany or Austria.

BLACK FRIDAY WEEK:
From 23 to 30 November 2026, selected smartphones and laptops are up to 20% off. Discounted products are marked in the shop. No code needed.

MANUFACTURER CASHBACK:
Some brands run their own cashback campaigns. Buy the product with us, then claim the cashback on the brand's website within the period the brand sets, usually 14 to 30 days, using your receipt or invoice. The brand pays the cashback, not Cyberport.

CAN PROMOTIONS BE COMBINED?
Only one promo code can be used per order. The Trade-in Bonus can be combined with 0% financing. Discounts that need no code, such as the Accessory Bundle Deal, can be combined with one promo code unless the promotion says otherwise. Promotions cannot be exchanged for cash.

PRICE PROTECTION:
Promotions apply to purchases made during the promotion period. Discounts are not applied to purchases made before a promotion started.
