STORES EXPERT KNOWLEDGE BASE — Specialty Bakeries & Pastry Cafés

HOLIDAY AND SPECIAL HOURS:
Some bakeries may operate on reduced hours or close entirely on German, Austrian, and Swiss public holidays such as Christmas (December 25–26), New Year's Day, Easter Monday, and regional bank holidays. Hours may also vary during local events or severe weather. Customers are advised to check the bakery's current hours via the app or website, which are updated in real-time. Push notifications may also alert customers of upcoming changes.

FRESH BAKING SCHEDULE:
All locations bake fresh on-site every morning. The full range of viennoiserie, patisserie, and breads is typically available from opening until mid-morning. Popular items such as croissants, pain au chocolat, and seasonal pastries often sell out by 11:00–12:00. Arriving early is recommended for the widest selection. Some locations offer a pre-order option via the app to guarantee availability.

SERVICES AVAILABLE PER LOCATION:
Services vary by location and may include:
- Click-and-collect (advance order via app)
- In-store seating and café tables
- Outdoor terrace seating (seasonal)
- Free Wi-Fi
- Take-away packaging
- Catering and corporate order fulfilment
- Custom cake consultation appointments

ACCESSIBILITY:
Most locations are equipped with accessibility features, including:
- Step-free entrances and wheelchair-accessible counters
- Allergen information displayed at the counter and available in the app
- Staff trained to assist customers with accessibility needs
- Braille menus available on request at selected locations

PARKING AND ACCESS:
Parking availability varies by city location. Most of our bakeries are in city centre or high-street locations well-served by public transport (U-Bahn, S-Bahn, tram). A small number of suburban locations offer adjacent parking. Drive-thru service is available at selected locations (see the app for details).

CATERING AND ADVANCE ORDERS:
We offer catering services for corporate events, office breakfasts, and celebrations. Custom celebration cakes require a minimum of 3 days advance notice. Large catering orders should be placed at least 5 days in advance. Please contact the store directly or submit a catering enquiry via the app.

FRANCHISE VS. COMPANY-OWNED BAKERIES:
Some locations are franchise-operated. Franchise locations may have minor variations in menu availability, pricing, or opening hours. All locations follow unified allergen, food safety, and quality standards. The app clearly identifies company-owned vs. franchise locations.

REGIONAL MENU VARIATIONS:
Certain bakeries may stock region-specific items. For example, southern German locations may carry Bretzel and Dampfnudel; Austrian bakeries carry traditional Kipferl and Strudel; Swiss locations may offer Gipfeli and Zürichbrötchen. Regional items are marked in the app menu.

ENVIRONMENTAL PRACTICES:
Our bakeries use compostable packaging and offer a discount to customers bringing their own reusable bags or coffee cups. Eco-certified locations are marked with a green badge in the app. We work with local suppliers to minimise food miles and reduce waste.

WASTE REDUCTION:
Unsold baked goods at end of day are offered at a reduced price in the final hour before closing. Any remaining items are donated to local food banks or composted. We do not throw away food.

STORE CONTACT:
Most individual bakeries do not have direct public phone lines. All enquiries should be made via the in-app chat or through our central support contact. For catering enquiries, a callback can be arranged.

TEMPORARY CLOSURES OR RENOVATIONS:
If a bakery is temporarily closed for refurbishment or operational reasons, it will be marked unavailable in the app with an expected reopening date where known.

LOYALTY PROGRAMME:
Customers can earn Pastry Rewards points at all eligible locations by scanning the app at checkout. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases. Some franchise locations may have limited redemption options — the app will notify you before checkout.

PET POLICY:
Service animals are welcome in all of our bakeries. Some locations with outdoor terraces are pet-friendly. Pets are not permitted inside the bakery unless they are service animals.

LANGUAGE SUPPORT:
Staff at German locations primarily speak German and English. Austrian and Swiss staff may additionally speak French, Italian, or local dialects. Allergen information and menus are available in English at all locations.

STORE EVENTS:
Selected bakeries host occasional events such as pastry-making workshops, seasonal tasting events, and artisan bread demonstrations. Events are promoted through the app, email newsletter, and in-store signage.

CUSTOMER REVIEWS:
Customers can leave store-specific ratings and reviews through the app. Ratings are reviewed by the regional quality team and help drive continuous improvement.

LOST AND FOUND:
Items left at a bakery may be held at the counter for up to 3 days. After that, unclaimed items are handed to the store's local management office. Please contact the store via the app to enquire about lost items.

NOISE AND ATMOSPHERE:
Our bakeries aim to provide a calm, welcoming environment. Background music is kept at a low volume. Some locations designate quiet seating zones, particularly in the morning. Please check the store profile in the app for details.

DELIVERY PARTNERS:
Selected locations fulfil delivery orders via third-party platforms (e.g., Lieferando, Uber Eats). Item availability may differ from the in-store menu for delivery orders. Check the relevant delivery platform for availability in your area.
