**Stores and Services KB — Cyberport Tech Assistant**

1. **When are your stores open?**
   Our stores in Germany are open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 until 20:00, and the store in Vienna from 10:00 until 19:00. Ask the Tech Assistant for a specific store's hours.

2. **Where are your stores?**
   Cyberport sells online and through a small network of stores in Germany and Austria, for example in Dresden, Berlin, Hamburg, Leipzig, Dortmund, Bochum, Munich, Cologne and Vienna. Ask the Tech Assistant for the address of a store.

3. **Are stores open on Sundays and public holidays?**
   No. Stores in Germany and Austria are closed on Sundays and public holidays, except on special shopping Sundays approved by the city, which are announced in advance. Regional public holidays differ between federal states.

4. **Which services are available in store?**
   All stores have a click & collect counter, product demo areas and expert advice. Most stores also offer a repair desk, device setup and data transfer, and a returns and warranty counter. Selected stores have a 24/7 pickup station. If there is no store near you, you can use all services by phone or online.

5. **What can the repair desk do?**
   The repair desk handles smartphone screen and battery replacements, laptop diagnostics and warranty repairs. Simple repairs on popular smartphone models can often be done the same day. For other repairs the device is sent to a service partner, and you are notified when it is ready.

6. **Can you set up my new phone or laptop?**
   Yes. Our device setup service transfers your contacts, photos and apps from your old phone, sets up email and accounts, and installs updates. On laptops we can install Microsoft 365 and antivirus software and transfer your files from an old computer.

7. **Can I bring my old devices for recycling?**
   Yes. Under German electronics law (ElektroG), larger electronics retailers, including online shops, must take back old electrical devices free of charge. You can hand in small old devices with no side longer than 25 cm, such as phones, chargers and headphones, in our stores, up to three items per device type and without buying anything. When you buy a new device, we also take back an old device of the same type free of charge.

8. **Do I need to delete my data before recycling or trade-in?**
   Yes. Back up your data, sign out of all accounts and reset the device to factory settings before you hand it in. You are responsible for removing personal data from devices you recycle.

9. **What about damaged or swollen batteries?**
   Do not charge or use a device with a swollen or damaged battery, and do not try to open it. Bring it to the store in a closed, non-flammable container and tell the staff at the entrance. Never put lithium batteries in household waste.

10. **What is a pickup station?**
   A pickup station is a 24/7 locker outside the store for click & collect orders. When your order is ready, you receive a code and can collect it at any time. The Tech Assistant can tell you which stores have one.

11. **Can I test products before buying?**
    Yes. Our demo areas let you try smartphones and laptops. Staff can show you the differences between models and help you compare specifications.

12. **Do you offer advice for business customers?**
    Yes. Business customers, schools and self-employed customers can get advice in any store or from the business sales team by phone. The team helps with bulk orders, business laptops, device setup and invoices with VAT.

13. **Are your stores accessible?**
    Most stores have step-free access, lifts between floors and accessible toilets. Staff can help you carry purchases to your car.

14. **Is parking available?**
    Our stores are in city centres, so they are easiest to reach by public transport. Public car parks are nearby.

15. **Can I order online and get advice in store?**
    Yes. You can reserve a product with click & collect and ask the store team for help when you collect it, for example with setup or data transfer.
